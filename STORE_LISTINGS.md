### Full Description

HDMechanicCRM is the only business management platform built specifically for auto repair shops that also handle scrap metal recycling. Stop juggling separate software for your mechanic work and scrap metal operations — HDMechanicCRM brings everything together in one powerful, easy-to-use application.

Whether you run a neighborhood garage, a towing company, a junk yard, or a full-service shop that handles both mechanical repairs and scrap metal recycling, HDMechanicCRM is designed around how you actually work.

**CORE FEATURES:**

**Work Orders & Job Management**
Create, assign, and track repair jobs from start to finish. Assign technicians, track labor hours, manage parts usage, and monitor job status in real time. Convert estimates to work orders with a single click. Kanban board view for visual job tracking. Recurring work orders for scheduled maintenance.

**Customer & Vehicle Database**
Maintain a complete customer database with full vehicle history. Track every repair, every invoice, every interaction. Service reminders keep your customers coming back. Customer portal lets clients view their own work orders and invoices online.

**Parts & Inventory Management**
Full parts tracking with quantities, costs, bin locations, and supplier info. Low stock alerts prevent costly delays. Kit assemblies, serial number tracking, supersession management, and cycle counts keep your inventory accurate. Multi-warehouse support with transfer tracking.

**Scrap Metal Operations**
Schedule scrap pickups, track metal types and weights, manage scrap vendors, and monitor revenue by material type. Purchase order management for scrap sales. Scrap metal inventory tracking with real-time pricing.

**Financial Management**
Generate professional invoices with multiple payment methods (Square, Cash, Check, Venmo). Accounts receivable tracking, credit memos, deposits, expense management, and AR aging reports. Job costing, budget vs. actual analysis, and revenue-by-category reporting. Bank account tracking and 1099 contractor management.

**CRM & Communications**
Email and SMS communication logging. NPS surveys and customer loyalty tiers. Lead tracking with follow-up automation. Appointment scheduling with calendar integration.

**Analytics & Reporting**
Business dashboard with real-time KPIs. Revenue charts, work order status breakdowns, scrap metal revenue trends. Technician efficiency reports and parts usage forecasting. QuickBooks CSV export for seamless accounting integration.

**Fleet & Compliance**
Track fuel records, hour meters, DEF/DPF emissions, DOT inspections, ELD logs, and tire records. TPM contracts, fleet management, and odometer-linked service scheduling.

**Warranty & Core Charges**
Manage warranty periods, track warranty claims, and handle core charge returns.

**Marketplace**
Browse and install workflow templates, integrations, and automation rules from the built-in marketplace. Extend HDMechanicCRM with community-built tools.

**Security & Administration**
Role-based access control (Admin, Manager, Technician, Front Desk). Encrypted passwords, HTTP-only session cookies, audit logging. Database backup and restore. Global search across all entities.

**Offline Support**
Progressive Web App with full offline capability. Perfect for mobile scrap pickups, field work, and areas with spotty internet. Data syncs automatically when connectivity returns.

**PERFECT FOR:**
- Independent auto repair shops
- Scrap metal recycling businesses
- Towing companies
- Junk yards and salvage operations
- Fleet maintenance departments
- Shops that do both mechanical work AND scrap metal

**WHY CHOOSE HDMechanicCRM?**
Unlike competitors charging $100-$600/month, HDMechanicCRM gives you everything in one place at an affordable price. No more paying for two separate systems. Self-hosted option available for shops that want full data control.

### Google Play Store

The Play artifact is the native WebView app in `android/` (package
`com.hdmechanic.crm`) — **not** the Bubblewrap/PWA path, which is legacy.
Build it with `./gradlew bundleRelease` (see `android/`; requires JDK 17 +
Android SDK). Purchases (Square) are completed in the external browser —
the app routes off-site navigations out — so Play Billing is not required.

**Listing URLs (live on the hosted deployment)**
- Privacy policy: `https://hdmechaniccrm.onrender.com/privacy`
- Terms: `https://hdmechaniccrm.onrender.com/terms`
- Refund policy: `https://hdmechaniccrm.onrender.com/refund`
- Account/data deletion: `https://hdmechaniccrm.onrender.com/data-deletion`

**In-repo assets (screenshots/ + app/static/icons/)**
- [x] Launcher icon 512x512: `app/static/icons/icon-512.png`
- [x] Feature graphic 1024x500: `screenshots/feature-graphic.png`
- [x] Phone screenshots 1080x1920: `screenshots/00-login.png` … `09-marketplace.png`
      (regenerate anytime: boot the app with `CRM_SEED_DEMO=1`, then
      `python take_screenshots.py`)

**Release keystore (generate once, keep offline backup!)**
```bash
keytool -genkeypair -v -keystore hdmechanic-release.keystore \
  -alias hdmechanic -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000
cp keystore.properties.example keystore.properties  # fill in values
./gradlew bundleRelease   # signed app-release.aab
```
