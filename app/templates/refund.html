Refund Policy

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

We want you to be satisfied with HDMechanicCRM. If the Service does not meet your needs, we offer a straightforward refund process.

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: If you are not satisfied with your paid subscription for any reason, contact us within 30 days of your first payment and we will issue a full refund.

1. Eligible Refunds

1.1 First-Time Subscribers

New paid subscribers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of their initial payment. No questions asked. We want you to try the Service risk-free.

1.2 Renewal Charges

If you were charged for a renewal and wish to cancel, contact us within 7 days of the renewal charge. We will issue a full refund for the renewal period and downgrade your account to the free tier.

1.3 Marketplace Purchases

One-time marketplace purchases (workflow templates, integrations, automation rules) are eligible for a refund within 14 days of purchase if the product does not work as described.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Email billing@hdmechaniccrm.net with your account email and a brief description of why you are requesting a refund
  • Or open a GitHub issue at github.com/ausgreekdev-cpu/HDMechanicCRM/issues
  • Include your account email, the date of the charge, and the amount charged

3. Refund Processing

  • Timeline: Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days of approval
  • Method: Refunds are issued to the original payment method
  • Currency: Refunds are issued in the same currency as the original charge
  • Processing fees: Stripe processing fees are not refunded (typically 2.9% + $0.30)

4. After a Refund

Once a refund is issued:

  • Your account is immediately downgraded to the free tier
  • You retain access to the free tier features
  • Your data is retained for 30 days, after which it is deleted
  • You may continue using the free tier without re-subscribing

5. Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Subscriptions older than 30 days (for first-time) or 7 days (for renewals)
  • Marketplace purchases older than 14 days
  • Custom development or consulting services
  • Domain name purchases (if applicable)

6. Disputes

If you believe you were charged in error, please contact us before filing a chargeback with your bank. We will resolve billing issues quickly and fairly. Chargebacks may result in temporary account suspension.
